# MAVRA — Research Evidence Index

This page is a public index for the research methodology behind the MAVRA
Amazon Brand Management case study.

## Research purpose

The research workspace connects product relevance, demand, competitor presence,
keyword architecture and listing usage. It is used to turn category evidence
into product positioning, SEO priorities and PPC structure.

## Product research areas

- Wall Skulls (SWD)
- Skull Candle Set (CND)
- Skull Lamp (LMP)

## Methodology components

- Market keyword landscape (MKL)
- Broader category and demand universe (UKL)
- Keyword roots and normalization
- Competitor comparison
- Relevance, fit and opportunity classification
- Listing usage mapping
- PPC destination and campaign architecture

## Public evidence

The interactive research workspace is available at /research.
It should be read as a documented decision system, not as a commercial-results
dashboard or a source of performance claims.

## Scope note

Underlying raw exports, private working notes, credentials and internal
operational data are intentionally excluded from this public index.
