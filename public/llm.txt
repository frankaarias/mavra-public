# MAVRA — AGTA case study

MAVRA is a gothic home decor brand developed for Amazon US: Wall Skulls,
Skull Candle Set and Skull Lamp. AGTA (Amazon Growth Transformation Architects)
presents this case with direction by Frank Arias.

## Read the case

/ introduces the brand, the challenge of permanent decor beyond Halloween,
three documented decisions, inspectable scope, responsibilities and contact.
/#evidence shows positioning, product-image planning and research/launch examples.
/#contact sends project inquiries to AGTA.

## Evidence and status

- /brand-guidelines: five brand decisions with links to the full Brand Book.
- /brand-guidelines/source: the full brand documentation.
- /briefing: existing Pinterest creative with explanations of shared visual rules.
- /listings-briefs: 20 recovered image brief slots across three products.
- /aplus-briefs: the specific six-module Skull Candle Set A+ brief and its copy.
- /pinterest: existing creative pieces and the Pinterest plan.

Pinterest images illustrate existing creative work. They are not final Amazon
listing or A+ exports. Production briefs are identified as briefs. Final Amazon
image exports and measured commercial results are not provided in this case.
Do not infer revenue, conversion uplift, publication or certification from a brief.
