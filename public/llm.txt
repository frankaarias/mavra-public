# MAVRA — Amazon Brand Management Case Study

MAVRA is a documented Amazon Brand Management case study by Frank Arias.
It demonstrates how brand strategy, Amazon retail execution, creative direction
and operating systems can be developed as one connected body of work.

## Case at a glance

- Role: Brand & Growth Lead
- Marketplace: Amazon US
- Scope: three product lines
- Built: 2026
- Product lines: Wall Skulls, Skull Candle Set, Skull Lamp

## What this case demonstrates

1. Market and customer discovery
2. Brand positioning and verbal identity
3. Creative direction and visual production systems
4. Amazon product merchandising: SEO, listings, image architecture and A+ content
5. Launch, PPC and creator-growth planning
6. Research and operating workflows that make decisions inspectable and repeatable

- 126 creators mapped
- 86 creator accounts manually verified

These figures describe documented scope and operating assets. They are not
commercial results, revenue claims or performance forecasts.

## About MAVRA

MAVRA is a premium dark-home brand conceived for permanent interiors rather
than seasonal decoration. Its brand platform, product architecture and Amazon
retail experience are connected through a single operating system.

## Public-facing intent

This website is a portfolio case study. It provides selected evidence of the
work and its methodology. It does not publish private production notes,
credentials, client data or unverified performance claims.
