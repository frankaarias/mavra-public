# AGTA / MAVRA

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Potential clients evaluating AGTA's Amazon brand management capabilities. The
first visit should explain the case; deeper exploration should reveal the work.

## Product Purpose

Present MAVRA as a documented case connecting brand strategy, Amazon content,
creative direction, market research and launch planning. Offer a contact path.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React/Vite site published at case.agta.io; repository frankaarias/mavra-public.
- Case study at `/`, resource library at `/brand`, with existing evidence routes.
- All public case content must support Spanish and English, with a shared toggle.
- Light and dark themes must remain legible and functional.
- The user maintains MKL/research and competitors. They are outside visual edits.
- Scope counts are not commercial results. Never invent outcomes or final assets.

## Brand Commitments

The existing fonts and palette are explicitly retained. No decorative gradients
in the light case experience. Preserve the agreed AGTA naming, collective voice,
and distinction between AGTA as author and MAVRA as the brand in the case.

## Evidence on Hand

Real Pinterest creative in `public/pinterest`, English document previews in
`public/shots`, bilingual case copy and linked resource pages. The Home explicitly
distinguishes production plans from final Amazon exports and measured results.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep visible keyboard focus, meaningful link labels, comfortable touch targets,
and reduced-motion support. Do not introduce autoplay or scroll-blocking effects.
